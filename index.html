About Our Housing Initiative

Upper Miramichi Non-Profit Housing is a longstanding, community-focused organization dedicated to offering modest, well-maintained independent living opportunities in central New Brunswick.

Operating for decades with local volunteer leadership, our mission is to ensure that community members have access to dignified, affordable shelter close to family, neighbors, and the rural roots they cherish.

We manage and sustain affordable residential units in the Upper Miramichi area.

Core Community Commitments

  • Affordable housing units
  • Carefully maintained properties
  • Run locally by volunteers
